THE INSPIREWISE QUESTIONNot only “What should I do?”

But “Who is making the choice — and what is shaping it?”

Because the career that looks right on paper can quietly stop fitting in real life.

THE PROBLEM

The “right” career works. Until it doesn’t.

We learn to choose from the outside in: marks, money, prestige, job titles, “safe” options, what everyone else seems to be doing. The result can be a career that is impressive on paper — and draining in practice.

InspireWise starts with the person making the decision.

OUTSIDE-IN

“What career should I choose?”

Then try to make yourself fit the answer.

INSIDE-OUT

“How do I actually work best?”

Then explore paths, environments and choices where that can thrive.

ABOUT INSPIREWISE

Human behaviour was always part of the work.

InspireWise was founded by Ashima Ahuja after more than a decade in consumer insights and brand strategy. The shift was from decoding how people behave in markets to helping people decode how they behave in their own lives and careers.

That is why the work sits at the intersection of behavioural insight, reflection, psychometric evidence and practical career decision-making.
